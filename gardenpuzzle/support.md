## How to play

1. Plant exactly one flower in every row, every column and every garden bed (the coloured areas).
2. Plants need room: two plants can never touch, not even corner to corner.
3. Tap a spot once to mark it with a pebble ("nothing grows here") and again to lift the pebble. Double-tap to plant. Drag across spots to lay pebbles quickly.

Every garden has exactly one solution and can always be solved by logic, without guessing.

## Watering cans and hints

- Planting in a wrong spot uses one of your three watering cans; the plant wilts and a pebble marks the spot.
- Out of water? Watch a short video to refill, or restart the garden.
- Hints show one spot that must hold a plant, with the reason. You get one free hint a day (three with Remove ads); a short video gives you another.

## Daily garden

A new puzzle every day, the same for everyone. Finish it on consecutive days to grow your streak. Turn on the evening reminder in Settings if you like.

## Remove ads

One purchase turns off the ads between gardens and gives you three free hints a day. Videos for extra hints or refills stay available but are always optional.

**Restore your purchase** on a new phone or after reinstalling: Settings → Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with.

## Problems?

- **Progress lost after reinstalling:** progress is stored on the device only, so a reinstall starts fresh. Your Remove ads purchase can always be restored.
- **No video available:** ads are not always in stock, especially offline. Try again in a moment, or restart the garden.
