_Last updated: 23 November 2026_

Plot & Bloom ("the app") is published by Cozylogic, a brand of Hybrid Aero Tech LLC ("we"), which is responsible for the data described here. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and the choices you have.

## The short version

- There are no accounts. We never ask for your name, email or phone number.
- Your progress (levels, streaks, settings) is stored only on your device.
- The app is free because it shows ads. Our ad partners use your device's advertising identifier and some device information to show and measure ads, subject to the choices you make.
- We collect anonymous usage events (for example "level completed") to improve the game.

## What is collected and by whom

| Data | Collected by | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Advertising identifier (IDFA on iPhone if you allow tracking, Android advertising ID), IP address, device model, OS version, coarse location derived from IP, ad interactions | Google AdMob and AppLovin (through AdMob mediation) | To show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance and prevent fraud |
| Usage events: app opened, level started or finished, hints and watering cans used, ads shown, purchases, daily garden finished; a random ID created on install; platform and app version | PostHog (our analytics provider) | To understand which levels are too hard or too easy, and how often ads appear, so we can tune the game |
| Purchase records for "Remove ads" (a store transaction ID and an anonymous app user ID; no payment details) | Apple / Google, and RevenueCat (purchase processing) | To unlock and restore your purchase |
| Notification permission and a scheduled local reminder (if you turn it on) | Your device only | To remind you about the daily garden |

We do not sell personal information. We do not collect contacts, photos, precise location or health data.

## Your choices

- **Ads consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland):** on first launch the app shows Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time from the app's settings or by reinstalling.
- **Tracking on iPhone:** after your first garden, iOS asks whether the app may track you across apps. If you say no, ads are not personalised with your IDFA. You see the same number of ads either way.
- **Android advertising ID:** you can reset or delete it in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
- **Notifications:** off unless you turn on the daily reminder. You can turn it off in the app or in your phone's settings.
- **Deleting your data:** uninstalling the app removes everything stored on your device. For analytics data linked to your install's random ID, contact us and include the app version and the approximate date you installed; we will delete it.

## Children

The app is rated for everyone but is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA). We do not knowingly collect data from children. Ad requests are not tagged as directed at children.

## Data transfers and retention

Our providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States, under their own safeguards (for example Standard Contractual Clauses). Analytics events are kept for up to 30 days. Ad partners keep data according to their own policies:

- Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- AppLovin: https://www.applovin.com/privacy/
- PostHog: https://posthog.com/privacy
- RevenueCat: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy
